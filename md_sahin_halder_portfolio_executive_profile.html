01 / Executive Overview

Multidisciplinary Precision in Infrastructure & Law

Md. Sahin Halder is a triple-qualified professional bridging civil engineering, spatial data/GIS, and international construction law. Operating at the intersection of complex commercial delivery and dispute avoidance, he advises on high-value infrastructure procurement, risk allocation, and cross-border contractual compliance.

He currently serves as Contracts & QS Manager at China State Construction Engineering Corporation (CSCEC) South Asia Branch, leading tender bids, joint venture representations, and FIDIC contract administration on flagship infrastructure works across Bangladesh.

Alongside his corporate leadership, he is the Founder of DRQS Consulting—a specialized advisory practice dedicated to construction dispute resolution, FIDIC claims, and statutory adjudication—and the DRQS School of Built Environment (DSBE), advancing professional capacity in Quantity Surveying and construction law.
